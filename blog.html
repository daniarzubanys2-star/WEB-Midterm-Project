Your first muscle-up in 12 weeks

The muscle-up looks like one big move, but it is three small ones: a high pull, a fast transition, and a strong press out. Most people stall at the transition because they pull straight up instead of pulling back toward the hips.

In this guide we break the move into drills you can practice twice a week: chest-to-bar pull-ups, straight bar dips, and the false grip on rings. You will also get a simple test to know when you are ready for the full rep.
